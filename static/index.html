Segments

each clip: waveform + words · ▶ plays just that clip · words highlight as it plays · click a word to jump
rescued 12% = that share of the clip is overlap audio recovered by SepFormer; the rest is untouched original (hover for seconds) impure 0.54 = voice match to this speaker's own voiceprint is below 0.55 — listen: crosstalk, wrong speaker, or a false alarm ✂ start/end = the first/last word runs into the clip edge — probably cut in half digits_uncertain = the clip has unaligned audio, but the text contains digits (87,000 · 10-15%) which forced alignment can't time — usually harmless. Dotted-underlined words are those untimed tokens. deva 76% / lat 24% = script mix of the transcript (Devanagari vs Latin). Hinglish keeps English words in Latin, so lat 0% on a clip where you hear English means the ASR transliterated it (प्रॉफिट instead of profit) — on pure-Hindi speech lat 0% is correct.
